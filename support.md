# TCGVendr Support

Need help with TCGVendr? Email us anytime at **support@tcgvendrapp.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does syncing work?**
TCGVendr syncs your data privately across your own devices using your personal iCloud account. It's automatic and doesn't require creating a separate account.

**How do I share a business with a partner?**
From the main menu, tap the business switcher, then "Create Shared Business…" to set up a new shared business and invite someone to it. Existing businesses can invite people from Settings.

**How do I manage or cancel my TCGVendr Pro subscription?**
Subscriptions are billed and managed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, to view, change, or cancel at any time.

**I found a bug or have a feature request.**
Email support@tcgvendrapp.com with details and we'll take a look.
