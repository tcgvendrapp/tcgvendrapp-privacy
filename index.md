# Privacy Policy for TCGVendr

**Last updated:** July 28, 2026

TCGVendr ("the app") is a personal and small-business inventory and transaction
tracker for trading card sellers. This policy explains what data the app handles
and how.

## What Data the App Collects

TCGVendr stores the information you enter yourself, including:

- Card details (name, set, number, condition, price, quantity, notes)
- Transaction records (purchases, sales, trades, payment method, amounts)
- Event records you create
- Photos you take or select of cards and transactions

TCGVendr does not require you to create an account, and does not collect
analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage tracking data of any kind.

## How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework, and
synced privately via your personal iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) — both across
your own devices, and, if you invite someone to a shared business, with that
person. TCGVendr has no servers of its own and cannot access your data; it is
never stored anywhere except your and your invitees' own iCloud accounts.

## Shared Businesses

TCGVendr lets you create one or more named "businesses" and invite specific
people, by your choice, to view and edit a shared set of inventory, transaction,
and event records for that business via Apple's iCloud sharing (CKShare). A
business is only shared when you explicitly invite someone to it, only the
people you invite can access it, and you can end that sharing at any time. Your
default "Personal" business is never shared with anyone.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

TCGVendr requests access to your camera and photo library solely so you can
attach photos of cards to inventory and transaction records. Photos are stored
with your other data as described above and are never uploaded anywhere outside
your own iCloud account or a business share you explicitly create.

## Subscriptions

TCGVendr offers an optional auto-renewing subscription ("TCGVendr Pro") that
unlocks additional usage limits and shared-business functionality. Subscriptions
are billed and managed entirely through your Apple ID via the App Store — we
never receive or store your payment card information. You can review pricing,
manage, or cancel your subscription at any time in your device's Settings app
under your Apple ID > Subscriptions.

## Third-Party Services

TCGVendr does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Card
identification/matching happens on-device using Apple's Vision framework and
does not send your photos to any external server.

## Data Deletion

You can delete individual records at any time from within the app. Deleting the
app removes its local data from your device; data already synced to your iCloud
account can be removed via your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Children's Privacy

TCGVendr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with
a revised "Last updated" date.
